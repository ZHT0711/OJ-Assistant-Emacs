# Third-party references

## OJ Assistant and OJ Assistant Plus

The platform adapters, problem importers, sample runner, and shared account
utilities under `src/` are adapted from OJ Assistant Plus and its OJ Assistant
upstream. The Emacs frontend and standalone bridge are maintained in this
repository. Their MIT license and upstream copyright are retained in `LICENSE`.

- OJ Assistant Plus — https://github.com/ZHT0711/OJ-Assistant-Plus
- OJ Assistant — https://github.com/lss233/OJ-Assistant

Runtime dependencies are installed by npm and retain their respective licenses.
This repository distributes its source and lockfile. Dependency source and
license files are provided with their npm packages.

## Link-practice reference

The link-practice module was independently integrated into OJ Assistant Plus,
with reference to elycode's URL-import workflow and Codeforces-to-Luogu public
problem mirror strategy. Reference revision: 2ad645b527aa23500970fed5127f514cf57f782c.

Source: https://github.com/YuXuaann/elycode
